About Mechatrolix

From Concept
Through Commissioning

Mechatrolix is a specialist industrial automation consultancy offering integrated EC&I, PLC programming, SCADA development, Robot programming and functional safety services to manufacturers, process plants and OEMs across Southern Africa and beyond.

We bridge the gap between engineering design and operational reality, delivering compliant, robust and maintainable automation solutions that meet international safety standards from concept through commissioning.

Our Services

Nine Disciplines.
One Accountable Partner.

Full-stack automation engineering from safety concept through commissioning sign-off.

01

Machine Risk Assessment & Functional Safety

ISO 13849, IEC 62061, IEC 61511. SISTEMA calculations, safety concept documentation, SIL/PLr verification and safety circuit design.

02

PLC / DCS Programming

Siemens TIA Portal (S7-300/400/1200/1500) / PCS7, Allen-Bradley Studio 5000 (ControlLogix/CompactLogix), Beckhoff TwinCAT 3, OMRON Sysmac. IEC 61131-3 compliant.

03

SCADA / HMI Development & SQL Integration

WinCC v7.x/v8.x, WinCC Unified/Professional, FactoryTalk View SE, Ignition, Adroit SCADA. Historian configuration and SQL Server integration.

04

Low Voltage EC&I Solution Specification

MCC design, panel specifications, field instrument datasheets, cable schedules in compliance with applicable LV standards and project codes of practice.

05

IEC Electrical Schematics

EPLAN Electric P8 and AutoCAD Electrical. Single/Multi line diagrams, loop diagrams, panel layouts, terminal schedules, instrument index documentation, parts list, BOQ and more.

06

Custom .NET Shop Floor Applications

Production tracking, operator dashboards, MES interfaces, downtime management and ERP integration using .NET Framework/C# and SQL Server.

07

FANUC Robot Programming

KAREL and Teach Pendant (TP) programming for welding, material handling, palletising and assembly applications on FANUC controllers.

08

Drives & Motion Control

Siemens SINAMICS, SEW Eurodrive, ABB ACS, Schneider Altivar, Danfoss VLT and Nidec Control Techniques drive selection, sizing and parameter configuration. Servo and motion control system design using Siemens S120 including multi-axis coordination, cam profiling and electronic gearing. Full site commissioning covering harmonic analysis, motor protection coordination and energy optimization for variable speed and precision motion applications across industrial processes.

09

Machine & Plant Commissioning / Project Management

FAT and SAT planning, site installation oversight, loop checking, handover documentation and subcontractor coordination through to sign-off.
