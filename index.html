Our Services

Nine Disciplines.
One Accountable Partner.

Full-stack automation engineering from safety concept through commissioning sign-off.

01

Machine/Plant Risk Assessment & Functional Safety

ISO 13849, IEC 62061, IEC 61508, IEC 61511. SISTEMA calculations, safety concept documentation, SIL/PLr verification and safety circuit design.

02

PLC / DCS Programming

Siemens TIA Portal (S7-300/400/1200/1500) / PCS7, Allen-Bradley Studio 5000 (ControlLogix/CompactLogix), Beckhoff TwinCAT 3, OMRON Sysmac. IEC 61131-3 compliant.

03

SCADA / HMI Development & SQL Integration

WinCC v7.x/v8.x, WinCC Unified/Professional, FactoryTalk View SE, Ignition, Adroit SCADA. Historian configuration and SQL Server integration.

04

LV EC&I Design, Electrical Schematics & Panel Building

Low voltage EC&I solution specification (MCC design, panel specifications, field instrument datasheets, cable schedules) and electrical design in EPLAN Electric P8 / AutoCAD Electrical: single/multi-line diagrams, loop diagrams, panel layouts, terminal schedules, instrument index and BOM. Panel building and electrical testing including panel assembly, continuity and insulation resistance testing, functional and loop checks, and Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) with documented test reports to IEC 61439, in compliance with applicable LV standards and project codes of practice.

05

IoT Integration & OT Cybersecurity

Industrial IoT integration connecting field devices, PLCs and edge gateways to on-premise and cloud platforms via MQTT and OPC UA for remote monitoring, condition-based maintenance and data-driven operations. OT cybersecurity aligned to IEC 62443: network segmentation and zoning, secure remote access, firewall and DMZ architecture, asset inventory, hardening and defence-in-depth for industrial control systems.

06

Business Intelligence & Reporting Applications

Custom BI and analytics applications that turn operational data into actionable insight: production tracking, downtime and OEE dashboards, fleet and telematics reporting, MES/ERP integration, and automated exception alerting. Built on .NET framework and SQL Server, with Power BI/reporting layers where required, connecting field and operational data (PLC, SCADA, GPS/telematics, weighbridge, warehouse) into unified dashboards for management and operations teams across manufacturing, mining, logistics, and process industries.

07

FANUC Robot Programming

KAREL and Teach Pendant (TP) programming for welding, material handling, palletising and assembly applications on FANUC controllers.

08

Drives & Motion Control

Siemens SINAMICS, SEW Eurodrive, ABB ACS, Schneider Altivar, Danfoss VLT and Nidec Control Techniques drive selection, sizing and parameter configuration. Servo and motion control system design using Siemens S120 including multi-axis coordination, cam profiling and electronic gearing. Full site commissioning covering harmonic analysis, motor protection coordination and energy optimization for variable speed and precision motion applications across industrial processes.

09

Machine & Plant Commissioning / Project Management

FAT and SAT planning, site installation oversight, loop checking, handover documentation and subcontractor coordination through to sign-off.
