Industries & Expertise

Where We Work

Mechatrolix brings proven automation engineering capability to every major industrial sector, from primary manufacturing to niche process industries across Africa and beyond.

Primary Industries

Manufacturing

General discrete and process manufacturing. PLC/SCADA integration, production tracking, OEE monitoring and MES connectivity.

Automotive

Robotic welding cells, body shop automation, assembly line integration and safety guarding design.

Mining & Mineral Processing

Conveyor control, crusher automation, plant-wide SCADA, reagent dosing, slurry management and safety-critical interlock systems.

Food & Beverage Processing

Hygienic process automation, CIP/SIP systems, batch management, recipe control, checkweigher integration and packaging line PLC.

Energy & Utilities

Oil & Gas

Pipeline SCADA, refinery DCS integration, hazardous area instrumentation (ATEX/IECEx), SIL-certified safety instrumented systems.

Power Generation & Transmission

Substation automation, power station auxiliary systems, MCC control philosophy, plant-wide SCADA and protection relay integration.

Water & Wastewater Treatment

Treatment plant SCADA, pump station telemetry, dosing control, remote monitoring and EC&I specification for plant overhauls.

Coal Handling

Stockyard automation, conveyor control, stacker/reclaimer PLC, dust suppression control and load-out weighing systems.

Heavy Industry & Materials

Rubber & Plastics

Injection moulding machine integration, extruder control, temperature profiling, recipe management and quality inspection systems.

Glass Manufacturing

Furnace control, annealing lehr automation, inspection systems and batch plant SCADA for float and container glass processes.

Cement & Construction Materials

Kiln control, raw mill automation, packing plant PLC, dust collection and weigh feeder integration for cement and lime plants.

Steel & Metallurgy

Rolling mill automation, furnace control, ladle handling, casting line PLC and high-availability SCADA for ferrous and non-ferrous processes.

Agriculture, Logistics & Other

Greenhouse Automation

Climate control, CO₂ dosing, automated venting, fertigation management and lighting control for horticultural operations.

Marine Applications

Vessel automation, ballast control, cargo management systems, alarm and monitoring panels and DNV-compliant engineering.

Warehousing & Distribution Centres

WMS integration, automated storage and retrieval systems (AS/RS), conveyor PLC and robot-based pick-and-place solutions.
